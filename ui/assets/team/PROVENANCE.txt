MOTION AND GREETINGS
ui/characters.js and ui/characters.css contain the full image in a 2:3 portrait frame. Normal human motion is subtle whole-character breathing, sway, thoughtful tilt, or a brief celebratory lift. A tap triggers a short whole-character response and an accessible greeting. The PNG itself has one pose: no separate limb motion, blinking, or redrawn facial expressions are claimed. Mino continues to use the existing capybara pose atlas.

IMAGE-ERROR FALLBACK ONLY
ui/character-rigs.js embeds the supplied simplified programmer.svg, graphic_designer.svg, artist.svg, copywriter.svg, and smm.svg. ui/character-rigs.css animates this fallback. It is revealed only by .character-image-error after the corresponding PNG fails to load; it is not selected by viewport size or used as the normal portrait.

The fallback preserves source SVG geometry, colours, and authored joint transforms, adding outer wrappers for small gestures/head motion and eye animation. The artist's brush receives the same outer motion as its hand. IDs are converted to data-rig-part handles to avoid duplicate DOM IDs. There are no scripts, event handlers, or external resource references in the embedded SVG source. The sibling editable SVG files and older team-rigs.js are not the active normal portrait implementation.

Both portrait and fallback motion respect reduced-motion settings, the Profile motion switch, hidden-page state, and offscreen visibility. No new image generation was used for these original PNGs. Source generate.py was not executed.
