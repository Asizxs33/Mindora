Mindora visual identity — 14 November 2026

Recognition: Mino's oversized glasses, small rounded ears and two front teeth.
Use the capybara mark as the main visual signature, not the old letter-M tile.

brand-mark.svg: standalone scalable mark, transparent canvas.
brand-logo.svg: horizontal mark and outlined Nunito Black wordmark.
favicon.svg: same capybara silhouette, suited to browser tabs.

Palette
Deep cocoa #422C24 — outlines, headings, wordmark.
Warm caramel #F5B66B — primary actions and Mino's face.
Soft cream #FFFAF0 — main background.
Paper #FFFEF9 — surfaces and controls.
Sage #4E7951 — positive feedback.
Lavender #D9C9F0 — Plus demo accents.

Typography: Nunito, with 900 weight for the wordmark and main headings.
The exported wordmark uses vector outlines and needs no font installation.
Leave at least one ear's width of clear space around the standalone mark.
Keep original proportions. Avoid gradients, extra shadows, or different glasses.
Small icons use the standalone mark; wider placements use the full wordmark.

Character system: Mino, Leo, Mia, Sam. Each atlas is a 1254 x 1254 transparent PNG
with four equal 627 x 627 cells: welcome, thinking, explain/support, celebrate.
Use brief reactions at meaningful moments; never make mistakes feel punishing.
Respect the learner's motion setting and the operating system's reduced motion preference.
Full character-generation prompts are in CHARACTER-PROMPTS-V3.txt.
