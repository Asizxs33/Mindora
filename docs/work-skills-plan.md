# Mindora · Two breaks, one weekly report

## Who needs it

Start with US operations coordinators aged 25–45 who prepare a weekly expense update while balancing shifts and family commitments. The immediate job is to turn a few receipts into a reliable total and a short message to a manager. An interrupted break can be the whole study session. The first five minutes should produce work someone can check.

Across participating OECD countries, 48% of adults who experienced barriers to adult learning cited time constraints related to work or family as their main barrier. This supports the scheduling problem. Demand for this particular offer among US coordinators still needs direct testing. [OECD, Trends in Adult Learning](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/trends-in-adult-learning_ec0624a6-en/full-report/why-is-participation-not-more-common_73893b53.html)

## Guest walkthrough

Open [the two-week exercise](https://mindora-livid-eta.vercel.app/#weekly-report). Day two is available immediately after the guided report so a reviewer can test the whole journey in one sitting. A learner can also close the browser and return to the same URL on the same browser profile. Guest drafts stay in that browser. Signed-in drafts use the same account workspace as the rest of the learning path.

| When | Learner action | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Today, first minute | Read the Friday request from the manager. Identify expense amounts in column B and the $100 budget. | Three expense rows of $24, $38 and $18. |
| Today, next three minutes | Use the worked range, total the rows and decide whether the team stayed within budget. | The app checks the range, total and decision separately. |
| Today, last minute | Read the resulting manager update. Stop for the day or open the next week. | Week one totals $80, or $20 under budget. |
| Tomorrow, first minute | Recall how to identify the first and last expense cells. Inspect the new delivery, supplies, printing and packing figures. | The exercise opens without a filled-in answer. |
| Tomorrow, next three minutes | Adapt the range for four expenses. Work out the total and make the budget decision independently. | $16 + $42 + $27 + $18 = $103. The old range misses packing. |
| Tomorrow, last minute | Read the checked report and explain the change to a manager. | The team is now $3 over budget. The view retains both checked reports on the same browser. |

Retrieval and spaced practice inform the design. Testing whether busy adults actually return and transfer the skill is a separate product experiment. [IES practice guide](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/WWC/PracticeGuide/1/Published)

## Offer and economics

Public examples are open for the jury. One personal AI course is free, including its lessons and saved progress. Further personal courses require the planned $9.99 monthly Plus subscription. Paid enrollment is closed until billing is connected.

The team reports a maximum observed token cost of $1.78 in its ten-course test. At that usage, subtracting token costs from $9.99 leaves $8.21 (82.2%) before other costs. A 70% contribution target leaves about $1.22 per subscriber-month for other variable costs beyond that token spend. Costs of free learners, retries, fees, infrastructure and support need measurement across cohorts. The observed batch is not a usage cap.

At the stated monthly price, $100M ARR would require about 834,168 paying subscribers for a full year before discounts. Model LTV using measured contribution and subscription lifetime, and CAC using acquisition spend per new payer. Both are unknown before a paid cohort.

## What to measure next

Recruit US operations coordinators with a real Friday report due. Observe whether they finish one report in a short break, then return the following day to produce the second report without help. Record completions and correct first attempts separately. Ask what they used previously and how long their actual task took. After checkout opens, measure paid conversion from the second-course offer, repeat usage, cancellations and full variable cost per paying subscriber. Report counts and denominators with each result.
